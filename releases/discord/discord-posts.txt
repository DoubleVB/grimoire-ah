# Profitmancer 1.3.9 is out

**New**
- **On-screen sale notification:** a pop-up like the cooldown one shows what sold and for how much, with sound, Move and Test buttons.
- **Your choice in the options:** sale text in the general chat, in a Profitmancer tab or not in chat, with or without the pop-up.

**Fixes & improvements**
- **Clearer sale messages:** item icon and clickable coloured name, coins lined up with the text, shorter wording.
- Sale messages keep their look after a `/reload` with chat history addons.
- **Sale notifications** now show up reliably: when an auction sells, you get the message with the item, quantity and price.
- New `/pfm gains saletest` command to check that the notification displays.

Update via CurseForge · Website: https://doublevb.github.io/grimoire-ah/

# Profitmancer 1.3.8 is out

**New**
- **Sale notifications**: when an auction sells, a message tells you what sold and for how much (listed price, before the AH cut). Optional dedicated chat tab and a Test button in the options (`/pfm options`).
- **Export straight to your shopping lists**: the Export button in the profession window sends the recipe's reagents to your AH shopping lists, with a confirmation pop-up in the middle of the screen. Shift+click still opens the copy window for Auctionator.
- **Listed items on the minimap tooltip**: how many items you have for sale at the AH (all your characters) and their total value.

**Fixes & improvements**
- Minimap tooltip is more compact and easier to read.
- Smaller red cooldown counter; the yellow glow on the minimap icon is now round and centered.

Update via CurseForge · Website: https://doublevb.github.io/grimoire-ah/

# Profitmancer 1.3.7 is out

**New**
- **Server types**: Profitmancer detects your server type (PvP, Normal, RP, RP-PvP) and uses the prices of that market.
- Cooldowns of characters on another server type are no longer mixed in; each character shows its server type in the options.

**Fixes & improvements**
- The options window shows which market the prices come from and warns you when they come from another server type.
- Clearer texts about price updates: Blizzard publishes prices once an hour, the updater fetches them within 15 min.
- The in-game guide is updated.

Update via CurseForge · Website: https://doublevb.github.io/grimoire-ah/

# Profitmancer 1.3.6 is out

**New**
- **Craft cooldowns** for every profession (transmutes, Mooncloth, Salt Shaker...): a notification when one is ready, a counter on the minimap button, and every character's cooldowns in its tooltip. Shared cooldowns are detected automatically.
- **Options window**: right-click the minimap button (or `/pfm options`). Tooltip lines, profession window display, notification position/sound/duration, AH tabs, window size, sales history length, characters followed, website links.
- **Skill-up chance**: the % chance of a skill point is shown next to each recipe.
- Recipes on cooldown show a tick or the time left in the profession window.

**Fixes & improvements**
- Item icons in the Sales history now work for every item (full WoW Forever catalog, EN/FR).
- Export button moved next to "Track Recipe", never covers text anymore.
- Smoother scrolling in the recipe list, lighter on performance.
- The in-game guide is updated with the new features.

Update via CurseForge · Website: https://doublevb.github.io/grimoire-ah/
